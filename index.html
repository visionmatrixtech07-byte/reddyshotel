1. Information We Collect (ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಸಂಗ್ರಹಣೆ)

When you place a food order or submit a catering inquiry through the Reddy's Pork Hotel website, we only collect the essential details you voluntarily provide: your Full Name, Mobile/WhatsApp Phone Number, Event Date, Guest Count, Selected Menu Items, and Delivery/Event Address. We do not sell or share your personal phone number with any third-party marketing agencies.

2. WhatsApp Ordering & Direct Communication

Our online ordering and catering forms connect directly with Proprietor S. M. Venkataramareddy's official business WhatsApp and phone lines (98443 10098 / 087221 33276). Your contact information is used strictly to confirm dish availability, share live catering price quotes, and coordinate timely food delivery or pickup in Srinivasapura.

3. Catering Service Booking & Advance Policy

  • Booking Confirmation: Catering orders for functions, weddings, village festivals, or bulk per-kg vessels should ideally be booked at least 24 to 48 hours in advance so we can procure fresh farm meat and grind fresh masalas.
  • Advance Payment: A nominal advance payment is confirmed directly with Proprietor S. M. Venkataramareddy in person or via phone to lock in bulk event dates.
  • Guest Count Adjustments: Any modifications to guest count or dish quantities should be communicated at least 12 hours prior to cooking.

4. Food Quality & Hygiene Commitment

At Reddy's Pork Hotel, every batch of Pork Masala, Pork Fry, Pork Boti Masala, Pork Boti Fry, Chicken Sambar, Chicken Kabab, Ragi Ball, Parota, and Rice is cooked fresh daily using clean potable water, quality cooking oil, and freshly sourced meat.
