1. Information We Collect (ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಸಂಗ್ರಹಣೆ)

When you place a food order or submit a catering inquiry through the Reddy's Pork Hotel website, we only collect the essential details you voluntarily provide: your Full Name, Mobile/WhatsApp Phone Number, Event Date, Guest Count, Selected Menu Items, and Delivery/Event Address. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking cookies or sell your phone number to any external marketing agencies.

2. WhatsApp Ordering & Communication

Our online ordering and catering forms connect directly with our official business WhatsApp number (+91 87221 33276) and phone lines (087221 33276 / 98443 10098). Your contact information is used strictly to confirm dish availability, share live catering price quotes, and coordinate timely food delivery or pickup in Srinivasapura.

3. Catering Service Booking & Advance Policy

  • Booking Confirmation: Catering orders for functions, weddings, or bulk per-kg vessels should ideally be booked at least 24 to 48 hours in advance so we can procure fresh farm meat and grind fresh masalas.
  • Advance Payment: A nominal advance payment is confirmed directly with Proprietor S. M. Venkataramareddy in person or via phone to lock in bulk event dates.
  • Guest Count Changes: Any modifications to guest count or dish quantities should be communicated at least 12 hours prior to cooking.

4. Food Quality & Hygiene Commitment

At Reddy's Pork Hotel, every batch of Pork Masala, Pork Fry, Pork Boti Masala, Pork Boti Fry, Chicken Masala, Chicken Kabab, Ragi Ball, Parota, and Rice is cooked fresh daily using clean potable water, quality cooking oil, and freshly sourced meat. We do not serve stale or reheated multi-day meat.
